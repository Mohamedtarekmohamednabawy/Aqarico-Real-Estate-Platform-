PURPOSE:
  Standalone FastAPI microservice that serves a pre-trained XGBoost regression
  model for real estate price prediction. The Node.js backend proxies requests
  to this service whenever a property price estimate is needed.

  Full-featured AI real estate consultant chatbot. Combines Google Gemini 2.5
  Flash (LLM) with agentic tool-calling to search the live MongoDB database,
  predict property prices using XGBoost, compare listings, and send email
  notifications — all in a conversational Arabic/English interface.
